## How a Denial Management Solution Improves Claim Recovery

Denied claims often go unpaid because no one owns the follow-up and appeal deadlines slip by. For small clinics, one missed deadline can mean revenue that was earned but never collected. A denial management solution gives the billing team one clear process to work every denial, from the first notice to final payment.

This article walks through a simple five-step workflow: sort denials by reason, find the root cause, prioritize by deadline and value, assign each denial to one person, and track every appeal outcome. It also covers how to review denial trends each month and which three numbers to watch: denial rate, appeal win rate, and days to payment.
