## How Neurology Billing Services Support Complex Neurological Procedures

A single missing modifier can turn a routine EEG claim into a denial that sits unpaid for months. This guide explains where complex neurology claims go wrong and how you can spot the problems early.

### What you'll learn

- Why neurology claims get denied: missing modifiers, expired authorizations, and late filing
- How billing support checks claims before the visit, before submission, and after payment
- A 5-step checklist for finding problems in individual and family therapy billing
- Monthly warning signs to track: repeated denials, rising days in A/R, delayed payments, and frequent coding corrections
- Signs that a practice may need outside billing support

**Audience:** Doctors, providers, and practice managers at small to mid-size US clinics.
